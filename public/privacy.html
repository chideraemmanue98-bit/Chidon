Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

At Chidon IQ, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use our website, application, services, and tools.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is strictly necessary to provide and improve our services, including:

  • Account Information: When you register an account, log in, or sign in using Google Auth, we retrieve your email address, display name, and profile picture url to personalize your profile dashboard.
  • Social Content Metadata: Analysis queries, tags, keywords, and text prompts submitted to check metrics or generate content.
  • Usage Logs: Metrics on tool execution times, feature access patterns, and diagnostic runs.

2. How We Use Google OAuth Data

Compliance Disclosure: Chidon IQ's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Our integration with Google Services (via Google Authentication) is designed exclusively for a secure and seamless identity handshake:

  • We access your basic Google Profile details (email, display name, profile photo) strictly for secure registration, login verification, and profile configuration.
  • We do not read, write, modify, or transmit any other Google account parameters.
  • We never sell or distribute your Google user profile data to any third-party marketing networks or external entities.

3. Data Storage and Retention

Your user profile settings, diagnostic history logs, and generated content drafts are stored securely using standard database parameters on Google Firebase Firestore Cloud instances. All active data transfers are fully encrypted under TLS/SSL protocols.

We retain your information as long as your account remains active. You can request account deletion or complete data purges at any time through your dashboard.

4. Cookies and Local Tracking

We use essential cookies and local browser storage (such as localStorage and sessionStorage) exclusively to maintain your active login session, persist your workspace preferences, and ensure high-speed asset loads. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising pixels.

5. Your Rights and Data Erasure

You maintain absolute ownership of your personal data. You have the right to:

  • View and export all analytical records and drafts saved under your profile.
  • Update or correct your personal display credentials.
  • Permanently delete your profile and purge all related databases.

6. Security Integrity

We employ state-of-the-art security measures to protect against the loss, misuse, and alteration of the data under our control. Although no security transmission over the Internet is 100% airtight, we work diligently with cloud-hosted isolation nodes to protect your records.
